## How to sign

You sign by adding a file to the repository in your first pull request, as described in `cla/README.md`.

- **Individuals** copy `cla/TEMPLATE-individual.md` to `cla/signatures/<github-login>.md`, fill it in and include it in the pull request.
- **Entities** list their people in a file under `cla/entities/`, which is Schedule A. Copy `cla/TEMPLATE-entity.md` to `cla/entities/<entity-short-name>.md` and list each covered GitHub login there.
- The founders of the company are covered by their own agreement with it.
