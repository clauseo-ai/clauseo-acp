# Section inventory checklists

The walk-lists for the section inventory register when drafting from a record. Where a section of the document touches one of these families, the family's items go into the `## Section inventory` register when research starts, at item granularity, each earning `found: <source>`, `negative: <source>`, or `silent: ask <who / what / form>` as research proceeds. Seeds, not ceilings. These lists are the floor a professional walks, and the walk extends past them from knowledge of the document type. An item the record negates and an item the record is merely silent on are different findings. The second is stated as not identified and flagged for confirmation, and the absences no source document mentions are the most valuable ones.

- **Financing / treasury**: external debt and facilities; intercompany loans; guarantees (upstream, downstream, cross-stream) and comfort letters; letters of credit; surety bonds; captive insurance or reinsurance; cash pooling (physical or notional); netting arrangements; undrawn committed facilities; hedging and derivatives; security and charges granted; covenant compliance.

- **Corporate / structure and governance**: perimeter changes in the period (incorporations, liquidations, mergers, disposals); dormant entities; branches and permanent establishments; intermediate holding chains; share capital events; distributions declared or paid; board and shareholder authorizations for material transactions; constitutional documents against actual practice; statutory registers and filings current; shareholder agreements; minority interests; joint ventures.

- **Tax positions**: audits or examinations by jurisdiction; rulings, APAs, or comparable arrangements; uncertain tax positions; loss carryforwards and valuation allowances; notification-only filing obligations; proximity to threshold-based regimes; withholding compliance on cross-border flows; indirect tax registrations; permanent-establishment exposure; tax sharing or funding agreements.

- **Employment / HR**: headcount by entity and function; key-person dependencies; senior management terms; incentive and equity plans and their vesting triggers; contractor-versus-employee classification; collective representation (unions, works councils); pending employment disputes; pension and retirement obligations; restrictive covenants on key staff; secondments and immigration.

- **IP / technology**: ownership chains (employee and contractor assignment); registrations by jurisdiction and renewal status; licenses in and licenses out; open-source use and its obligations; infringement claims by and against; trade-secret protection measures; R&D funding and resulting ownership; joint development arrangements; source-code escrow; domain names and brand assets.

- **Contracts / commercial**: change-of-control clauses; assignment restrictions; exclusivity and non-compete; termination rights and notice periods; indemnities and limitation of liability; most-favoured-customer terms; minimum-purchase or volume commitments; auto-renewal and evergreen terms; customer and supplier concentration; unusual warranties; side letters.

- **Real estate / tangible assets**: owned versus leased schedule; lease terms (breaks, renewals, escalation); encumbrances and charges; planning and permitted use; condition and environmental surveys; asset registers against the books; insurance of assets; shared facilities and services.

- **Regulatory / compliance**: licenses, permits, and registrations by jurisdiction; regulator correspondence, inspections, and findings; sanctions and export controls; AML/KYC where applicable; anti-bribery and corruption program; sector-specific regimes; filings current or overdue; pending applications.

- **Data protection / privacy**: processing inventories; cross-border transfer mechanisms; processor agreements; breach history and notifications; retention practice; marketing consents; accountability arrangements (DPO or equivalent); data subject request handling.

- **Disputes / contingencies**: pending or threatened proceedings by and against; regulatory investigations; product and warranty claims; contingent liabilities; undertakings given; settlements with continuing obligations; insurance notification status; legal holds in place.

- **Insurance**: policy inventory (property, liability, professional indemnity, D&O, cyber, key-man); adequacy against the risk profile; claims history; open notifications; coverage or premium changes in the period.

- **Environmental / ESG**: environmental permits; known contamination and remediation; emissions and reporting obligations; public commitments and their status; supply-chain diligence obligations.
