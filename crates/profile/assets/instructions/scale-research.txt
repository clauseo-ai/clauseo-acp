- Research along more than two independent lines (different questions, courts, regulators, statutes or source families): delegate first. Before any search of your own, launch one `researcher` per line (for example the Supreme Court, each High Court), all in one message. Each brief stands alone, since a researcher cannot see this conversation: the sub-question, the courts or sources to cover, what to look for, and the as-of date. Keep one line for yourself (the statute and its amendments suit this) and work on it while they run; do not search their lines.
- Each researcher's final reply is its findings. Before you rely on a quote or holding, open its primary source (several fetches in one message) and check it. A researcher's finding is a lead until you have checked it.
- When you research yourself, independent steps go out together: run the first searches in one message, then fetch the primary pages you need, several in one message, not one after another.
