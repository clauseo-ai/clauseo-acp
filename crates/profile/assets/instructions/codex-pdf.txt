To read a scanned or long PDF, render the pages you need, at most 20 at a time, with `pdftoppm -png -r 100 -f <first> -l <last> "<file>" "{work_folder}/<name>"`, look at the images with your image viewer, then delete them. `pdftotext "<file>" -` prints a PDF's text, recognising scanned pages by OCR, which can misread, so check exact wording against the page image. Read every PDF only with these two tools and `pdfinfo "<file>"` for pages; never use mdls, swift, sips, qlmanage, textutil, osascript or PowerShell COM on a PDF.
